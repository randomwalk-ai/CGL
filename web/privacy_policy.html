1. Purpose

The purpose of this Privacy Policy is to communicate Random Walk's commitment to protecting personal information of users, customers, employees, and third parties. It ensures compliance with global privacy laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and other applicable data protection regulations.

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All data collected, processed, or stored by the Life Lab mobile application.
  • Employees, contractors, and third-party processors handling data on behalf of Random Walk AI.
  • All systems, applications, and platforms where data resides.

3. Definitions

  • Personal Data: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Data Subject: An individual whose personal data is processed.
  • Processing: Any operation performed on personal data (collection, storage, use, transfer, deletion).
  • Controller / Processor: Roles defined under GDPR describing data ownership and processing responsibilities.

4. Policy Statements

Random Walk AI operates under a philosophy of data minimization. The Life Lab application was engineered to provide a fully offline, privacy-first simulation environment.

4.1 Data Collection

The Life Lab mobile application processes and stores gameplay data entirely locally. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data (such as names, email addresses, IP addresses, location data, or device identifiers).

Non-personal game configurations, tutorial progression, and high scores are stored locally in the application's private storage directory.

4.2 Data Usage

Any information stored locally is used solely to maintain game state, track achievements, and display local high scores. Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, Random Walk AI does not use your data for advertising, user tracking, profiling, or analytical reporting.

4.3 Data Sharing

No data is shared with third parties, as no personal data is collected.

If you choose to share your simulation statistics or graphics via the in-game "Share" button, the system triggers the standard Android OS share sheet. All image generation and text sharing are executed locally on your device, and the data is shared directly with the applications you select.

4.4 Data Retention

Local game state is retained indefinitely in your device's local storage to preserve your configurations and high scores. You may securely delete this data at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing the application's data via Android System Settings.

4.5 Data Security

Although the app processes no personal data, we enforce secure defaults inside the application structure. We compile our application configuration in compliance with modern secure-storage standards for Android to prevent local file tampering.

4.6 Data Subject Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA, users have rights regarding their personal data. Because Life Lab does not collect or transmit any personal data, Random Walk AI does not hold any information to access, rectify, or delete. You can exercise complete control and deletion of all local app files directly through your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

4.7 Children's Privacy

Life Lab does not target children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area) for the collection of personal data, nor do we knowingly collect any such data. The simulation is safe and operates fully locally for all age groups.

4.8 Breach Notification

In the event of a security audit revealing vulnerabilities, or any incident involving Random Walk AI's broader operational systems, we will inform affected parties in accordance with global regulations. Since Life Lab operates fully locally, there is no central database or cloud storage susceptible to data breaches.

5. Roles & Responsibilities

  • Data Protection Officer (DPO): Oversees compliance with privacy laws and addresses user inquiries regarding data policies.
  • IT & Security Teams: Maintain secure development and distribution pipelines for mobile packages.
  • Employees & Support Agents: Address client queries with proper respect for privacy, ensuring zero collection of unsolicited personal details.

6. Monitoring & Compliance

Privacy audits of our code bases and dependency maps are conducted annually. This ensures that no third-party integrations or updates inadvertently introduce telemetry or data logging without explicit customer consent.

7. Enforcement

Violations of this privacy policy by Random Walk AI personnel, partners, or contractors will result in disciplinary action up to termination, alongside any appropriate legal or regulatory penalties.

8. Review & Maintenance

This Privacy Policy will be reviewed annually and updated to reflect evolving legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.
